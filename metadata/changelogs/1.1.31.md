# 1.1.31

## Changes

- The Standard Filling Agent can now be made at the Tabula Vitae (tier 3) from a Weak Filling Agent, a Strengthened Catalyst, Glowstone Dust and Chorus Fruit.
- The Orb Vitae Link now charges five times faster. Several Links on one altar share that rate, so one Link is all you need.
- Crystallum Fractura now scales Fortune on each crystal's own aspect in the chunk, so a Ruina-heavy chunk gives more Ruina crystals. Geode clusters use the stone's biased aspect, or Raw.
- The Gathering now stores items anywhere in its range straight into the chest on its stone, so items stuck behind blocks are no longer missed. It also keeps filling the chest while its owner is offline.

## Fixes

- The Ritual of Lost Souls now drops Spiritus Essence that your Spiritus Gem absorbs on pickup, instead of an unstackable item that filled up your inventory.
- The Dungeon Alternator no longer stops itself when redstone dust is connected to it, and its linked blocks no longer stay powered after it stops.
